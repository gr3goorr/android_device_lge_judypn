
Device configuration for LG V40 ThinQ
=========================================

The LG V40 ThinQ (codenamed "judypn") is a high-end smartphone from LG.

It was announced in October 2018. Release date was October 2018.

## Device specifications

Basic   | Spec Sheet
-------:|:-------------------------
SoC     | Qualcomm SDM845 Snapdragon 845 (10 nm)
CPU     | Octa-core (4x2.8 GHz Kryo 385 Gold & 4x1.7 GHz Kryo 385 Silver)
GPU     | Adreno 630
Memory  | 6 GB RAM (LPDDR4X) on every variant
Storage | 64 GB or 128 GB (UFS 2.1), expandable via microSDXC up to 2 TB (shared SIM slot)
Shipped Android Version | Android 8.1 (Oreo), upgradable to Android 10, with LG UX 7.0+
Battery | Non-removable Li-Po 3300 mAh battery
Charging | 18W wired (Quick Charge 3.0 / USB Power Delivery 2.0), 10W wireless (Qi)
Display | 3120 x 1440 pixels, 19.5:9 ratio, 6.4 inches, P-OLED capacitive touchscreen, HDR10, 16M colors (~537 ppi density), including notch, Corning Gorilla Glass 5, Always-on display
Rear camera | Triple camera: 12 MP (f/1.5, 27mm standard, 1/2.55", 1.4µm, dual pixel PDAF, 3-axis OIS) + 12 MP (f/2.4, 52mm telephoto, 1.0µm, 2x optical zoom, PDAF, OIS) + 16 MP (f/1.9, 16mm ultrawide, 1/3.1", 1.0µm, fixed focus), LED flash
Front camera | Dual camera: 8 MP (f/1.9, 26mm standard, 1/3.2", 1.4µm) + 5 MP (f/2.2, 21mm wide, 1.4µm)
Video   | 4K@30/60fps, 1080p@30/60/240fps, HDR video, gyro-EIS, 24-bit/192kHz stereo sound recording
Audio   | 32-bit/192kHz Hi-Fi Quad DAC, tuned with Meridian Audio, Boombox speaker
Fingerprint | Rear-mounted
Sensors | Accelerometer, gyroscope, proximity, compass, barometer
Bluetooth | 5.0, A2DP, LE, aptX HD
Wi-Fi   | 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, dual-band, Wi-Fi Direct, DLNA
GNSS    | GPS, GLONASS, GALILEO
NFC     | Yes
USB     | USB Type-C 2.0
3.5mm jack | Yes
Dimensions | 158.8 x 75.7 x 7.6 mm
Weight  | 169 g
Build   | Glass front and back (Gorilla Glass 5), aluminum frame, IP68, MIL-STD-810G compliant
Models  | V405, LM-V405, LM-V409N
